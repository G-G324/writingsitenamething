Short Story: Creepin'

“They keep creepin’ towards the door.” Those were the words that echoed through Violet’s mind as each second pierced through her. They were coming for her, she knew it. She didn’t know why they were here or how they got to her home, but that didn’t matter at this point. All that mattered was staying away from them.

The heavy, metallic footsteps continued to steadily pass by. Eventually, one of the mechanical monstrosities was going to push the door open and see the cowering woman in the corner of the room, and the thought of what might happen then sent a shiver down her spine. Too nervous to move, the brunette simply sat there.

The only source of comfort being, strangely enough, a cartoon character plastered on the screen of a television on the other end of the room. It was a rather odd companion, but from her job working to watch these monstrosities, she was not surprised in the slightest and did not question how the TV could be on during the power outage nor why the character looked so much identical to her. To her regret, she had worked at that job long enough to know that wherever the robotic beasts traveled, figments of the mind were bound to follow.

Another creak was heard, and the brunette girl pressed herself further into her corner. The loud, metallic footsteps grew closer and closer just as the scent of rotten flesh started to rise. The cartoon girl waved her arms frantically, matching Violet’s worried expression and catching her attention. The cartoon pointed repeatedly to the closet with panic. Violet understood. WIthout a second thought, she scurried there and quickly closed the shutter door behind her. And just in time too, for immediately after, the door to her room burst open to give way to one of the robotic hunters.

Violet watched through the closet door’s blinds as the beast stopped in front of her. It almost resembled a robotic humanoid fox. Withered and aged.

It surveyed the room, evident by the lights projecting from its eyes and planting two moving circles on the walls and ceilings as Violet tried to stay still. Violet could feel her own nervous heartbeat skip and tried her best to hold back a scream as the beast’s eyes highlighted the closet door.

It remained still, eyes still locked in her direction, and the brunette sank further back behind the hanging clothes. She tightly shut her eyes and waited, then froze as the beast suddenly let out an ear-splitting roar. Violet clung onto the clothes in front of her, savoring her breath, fully believing it to be the last she’d take, but… nothing happened. The door to the closet was not touched, though she could still hear the mechanical monster moving about. A crash almost instantly followed the footsteps, and the girl opened her eyes again to see the beast searching for her in the room, pulling and knocking down anything and everything in its view.

Violet prayed that the thing wouldn’t find her as the monster’s search carried on, pulling and flipping her bed on its side to knocking down her dresser and the television her cartoon companion resided in. The sound of crashing and shattering glass filled the room, but unfortunately, the voiceless cartoon was unable to compete and banged against the screen to get Violet’s attention. A success! Violet looked at the cartoon with fear and uncertainty filling her eyes, but the beast roared once again and she anxiously shifted her eyes back to the monster.

Violet turned her attention back to the cartoon girl who was attempting to mouth something. After precious seconds spent analyzing her mute companion’s lips, Violet finally understood. At least, she had hoped so. “Light.”

The girl desperately scavenged through her closet, searching for the flashlight she stored in the closet prior. Why light was needed, she wasn’t sure, but she hoped her lip-reading was accurate. A light shone through the shutters and at last, the flashlight was found and she grabbed it with shaking hands. She noticed the silence on the other end of the closet door. The strong smell of a rotten corpse still remained, so it couldn’t have left. In an instant, the brunette’s blood ran cold. It pained her that she didn’t notice sooner, and the scent of death snuck past her nose and tickled her soul. Her clumsily loud searching had attracted the beast’s attention. “I’m dead,” she thought to herself, the latter word weighing down on her like a forty-ton cinderblock.

Violet looked to the cartoon once more, pleadingly. What was she supposed to do with a flashlight? The cartoon mouthed something again, though this time Violet was even more unsure of what was being said. The beast took a step forward and the cartoon immediately pointed to its own eyes. Before Violet could turn back to the monster in front of her, it already began its sprint towards the door, and within a second, the shutter door had swung open.

The mechanical monster let out an ear-splitting screech and wasted no time in reaching an arm to grab her. The frightened girl emitted a yelp of her own, her thumb instantly moving up to turn on the flashlight with not even a second to spare before the thing’s hand reached her. The light hit its eyes and the beast stopped in its tracks and stumbled back, letting yet another screech sound. As it covered its eyes, the pointed hook replacing one of its hands gleamed in the light. The cartoon’s plan worked! The animated character wiped the sweat from its brow and exhaled a silent breath, evident from the three swirling lines coming from its mouth.

Violet didn’t feel anywhere near as relieved as her cartoon counterpart, and her own shaky breaths only grew heavier as she stared at the staggering beast before her, still not believing she still drew breath.

Why was it here? Why were they here? The question and possible answers flooded Violet’s mind, but she willed away the frightening thought and sprinted past the blinded beast before her and through the threshold that once held a door. She had to get out of the house fast.

She bolted to the staircase to run to what would hopefully be safety on her left, but a beam of light shot out from the window, acting as a border between the girl and her route to freedom. Violet couldn’t help but let out another silent cry of surprise as she attempted to halt her feet, only to fall back onto herself. They must have been patrolling outside of the house as well. Too nervous to stand, she supported herself on her elbows and decided to just wait for the ray of light to pass.

“Come on…” she urged, not fully realizing that she was thinking aloud. Six seconds passed before the light finally parted, but to the girl, the time was an agonizing six hours. That fox had probably recovered by now, and without a need for a cue, the beast affirmed her fear with a single roar.

She heard the wooden floor strain under the weight of its feet as its hooked hand pierced into the threshold. Its head soon followed and looked down the path opposite to the frightened girl. Violet needed no further motivation and scrambled to her feet to head down the staircase while she still could. Halfway down, she spotted another one of the mechanical beasts patrolling her living room. Bulkier than the fox, it seemed to be in better condition than its rusted associate, which was terrifying in its own right.

With heavy steps, it stomped away into the next room. Loud clangs grew more and more distant. The exit was so close! Her thoughts became silent, ecstatic prayers, pleading to any and every deity she could think of, hoping that at least one of them would get her through this alive.

She lightly pressed her foot onto the next stair. Maybe she could sneak by. No sign of it hearing her so far, but like Violet, these rickety old steps were infamous for not dealing well under pressure. A misstep could easily mean the end of her. She gripped onto the rails and continued to carefully shimmy her way down the stairway. So far, not a creak to be heard. “Almost there.”

A spotlight beamed down upon her from the top, and like a deer in headlights, she froze. She didn’t bother to turn around, for she already knew the cause of the sudden light. That damned fox.

As though stricken with thunder, a sudden shiver and spasm coursed through her as the fox beast’s deafening roar met with her ears again. Yet she did not move. Her feet were glued to where they rested. Despite this, her heart was the most active, frantically banging against her chest faster and faster in a desperate attempt to break free from her body and do what her feet could not.

She looked down at the shadow projected from the beast’s vision as she noticed something odd. It was her shadow. It stood in a different manner from its paralyzed owner and pointed repeatedly to the door ahead, urging her to run. If silhouettes could shout, this one definitely wouldn’t be silent.

Violet suddenly found the strength to move and wasted no time in using the gifted mobility to hurry down the rest of the stairs and rush for the gateway out.

The quick shudders that vibrated through the floorboards and shook the room proved the bulky robot had heard the fox’s cry. A quick arm shot out towards the handle of the door as a new spotlight shone against her side. She was running out of time!

With a twist and forceful tug, the front door swung open, and in a single motion she passed the threshold and locked the beasts inside.

And everything grew still.

There was no thudding from the other side of the door, nor was there a crash or cry to be heard. Even the wind refused to howl in the cool night. Violet leaned against the wooden surface in an attempt to steady her breath. She couldn’t believe she actually made it out. If only she had a phone at hand to call someone. Still, the lack of any movement on the other end was… odd. Almost unnerving.

Now to find help. But she was shocked at what she saw upon turning from the entrance. Instead of the trees and grass the outdoors would promise, was her own room. Nothing was broken, nothing knocked down, as if the fox hadn’t torn through the room prior.

She stared, more than puzzled by the sudden change in scenery. “How…” This didn’t make sense. She had just left her home. How was she back?

Violet’s ears finally picked up a sound. Not the beasts, no. In fact, it sounded just like her. She followed the quiet noise to see herself sitting, curled up in a corner of the room.

“No…” Violet’s eyelids suddenly fell heavy, her lungs forgot how to function, and she collapsed onto the floor staring back at the silently whimpering girl in the corner.

Violet awoke from her dream, panting, clutching the sheets of her bed. She quickly surveyed her room. Nothing out of the ordinary. No toppled dressers, no living cartoons, robots, or… other hers.

“I’m quitting that job.”

Film Review: We Can Be Heroes

Part 1. It’s not Sharkboy and Lavagirl 2

We Can Be Heroes is an interesting production. Not because of the fact that it’s one of the many Netflix-only films or the fact that one of the characters is a someone I swear is a discount Homelander from The Boys, but it’s interesting solely because of the marketing.

We Can Be Heroes is a movie about new fledgling heroes saving their parents and the world from an alien threat that looms over the city. It also apparently is set in the world of the classic kids' film, Sharkboy and Lavagirl, and the duo were marketed as one of the main things drawing people into this film. In some countries, the film is even called The Adventures of Sharkboy and Lavagirl 2, so imagine, for a moment, the confusion and disappointment some audience members had when they discovered just how little the characters actually play a part here. They are cameos at best, and some critics on Rotten Tomatoes were not pleased by this.

I don’t know how relevant that was. I just find it fascinating. Anyhow, now with that out of the way, let’s finally get to the movie.

Part 2. Actual Intro

The movie begins with the mighty, miraculous Miracle Guy, a new character who seems to be this world's version of Superman in a Homelander cosplay. He and his partners sense something is amiss, and discount Homelander flies out into space to see what’s wrong. What he finds? A big ol’ spaceship. Just blasting some sort of energy ray at him. (Yes, that is what he says, and I do not know why I love that quote.) As Miracle Guy stops to take a selfie before he no doubt puts the hurt on the intergalactic fiend, he realizes there wasn’t just one ship… but a thousand.

So anyways, they started blasting and Miracle Guy falls right out of the sky as a decently dramatic narration of the heroes, well, falling is spoken in the background.

We cut to our actual main character, Missy, who’s the daughter of the retired leader of the same group Miracle Guy is from: a hero group with the uninspired but technically not taken name, The Heroics. He, of course, isn’t taking the news of his most powerful hero going down very well and is clearly on edge, so after dropping Missy off for school, he goes to the Heroics headquarters, where he is forced to take up the role as a hero once again. What are his powers? I’m not sure. They don’t really say. Also, Sharkboy and Lavagirl are a part of the Heroics here and are mentioned as married in this scene, so shippers rejoice!

Meanwhile, agents of the Heroics believe that Missy may be a powerful fit for their group of fledgling heroes… who study in a vault. For some reason… But it’s here we see the rest of the main cast, which was way more than I initially thought.

Part 3. The main characters

Wheels Whose power is that he’s in a wheelchair.

Noodles: A kid who can stretch his body Mr. Fantastic style.

Ojo: A character who doesn't speak and tells the future through her drawings. Yet no one has picked up that she is just drawing the future despite it being inferred that they have all known each other for a while. The other cast hypes her up by saying she’s so talented she puts even Van Gogh to shame, but they did not need to. She draws like a child, and I can only imagine that the characters do this every time someone wants to see her drawings.

Acapella: Who can levitate objects by singing.

Slo Mo: Whose power is that he’s so fast that he’s... slow? I don’t… some of these powers don’t make any sense, but he’s used as a gag character, constantly being behind the cast.

Facemaker: Shapeshifts his face.

Fast Forward and Rewind: Twins who can… their name. They can solve a lot of the issues in the film as the writers never gave them any limit to when they can rewind time, so they just kind of forget they can do that so the plot can continue. It will get very annoying if you think too hard.

Wildcard: Any power in the world, but random.

Guppy: Daughter of Sharkboy and Lavagirl and is the most reference to them you’ll see. She can use water as a weapon or for utility so long as it's present in the room. She’s a waterbender basically. When she uses her powers, the theme to the Sharkboy and Lavagirl movie will play, which I think is a cute addition.

And Missy: The main main character, whose power is absolutely nothing.

Part 4. Basic plot

I thought there were way less main characters. Anyways, Missy realizes that the art girl is drawing the future, they all witness the heroes get defeated by the aliens on the news. Sharkboy gets one-shot, Lavagirl hits her own teammate, the time twins argue about whether or not to watch their parents get captured again, Missy’s dad charges in like an idiot and immediately gets captured, and all hope is lost as the kids are instructed to stay inside while these aliens wreak havoc on the world.

Now with that strong, if not a little wacky, start, it is up to Missy to gather her new peers and stop this looming threat of aliens and prove to herself that you don’t need powers to be a hero.

The film is dumb, cheesy, classic Robert Rodriguez action with surprisingly little that’s changed from his style over the years. Even the CGI is still weirdly unchanged from how it was in his earlier works, but it doesn’t distract. If anything, it adds to the delightful cheesiness present in his movies. The characters are all likable in their own right.

However, it isn’t all good. While some scenes are clever, there is a big flaw. The plot falls apart near the end. Otherwise, it’s a fun, enjoyable, cheesy film about superheroes.

I give it a 7/10.

Journalism: The Josh Swain Battle

It was a battle of the Joshes on April 24th this year, as several people with the same name gathered for a battle royal.

In 2020, a 22 year old Josh Swain messaged several sharing his name on Facebook with a single proposition: a battle between each and every one of them. The winner of this free-for-all would earn the right to be the one true Josh Swain while the losers would have to forfeit the right to their former names. At pure random, the coordinates and date were set.

"It was entirely a joke," says the original Swain. "I see people making memes about me and my name, and it's the weirdest feeling ever." Joke or not, Swain had everybody's attention. After Swain posted screenshots of the group chat to Twitter, more and more people had their eyes set on the event. Word spread onto other websites. Soon even an entire fanbase based on the website, Reddit, was formed, gaining over 3,000 members. The internet appropriately dubbed this event the Josh Swain Battle.

One issue arose when someone found the coordinates given were actually those of someone's home who had no clue of the Swainbowl. Soon, there was too much attention to not see this joke through to the end. After this, the original Swain changed the coordinates and formed a proper plan. Those curious would come to him for updates.

"To be totally honest, I did not intend for this to actually happen. I picked those coordinates at random as the geographical center of the U.S... I do intend to be there, however I don't want to make it a burden on some innocent landowner nearby."

Following this, a sign was formed near the original coordinates stating that the Josh Swain Battle would not take place there and to go to the new location.

The most recent update regarding the event was four days before the big battle. On a Reddit post, the original Swain posted a verification form for attendees to fill out as well as updated rules. The famous battle would be a rock paper scissors game that would follow a pool noodle battle between anyone with the name. Those who only had the first name of Josh would also be allowed to partake. Specifically, in the pool noodle battle.

Additionally, a donation fund would also be present, with part of the money going to a medical center. He ends the post by saying “Please consider donating to show the world how the internet can turn an exercise in absurdity into something beautiful.”

In the end, only one other person bearing the name Josh Swain arrived to the challenge, but to those watching, that did not disappoint. With 950 live spectators at the scene, and 50 other attendees holding the first name of Josh, this unusual battle royal was still in some ways a success.

10 Best Teams In Fighting Games

Fighting games usually follow a simple tried and true method. Two fighters on screen with the only goal being to beat the stuffing of the other person. Depending on which game it is, there might even be eight fighters, but the point still remains that near the end of the match, only one of them can be the victor. That is why it’s so refreshing when a fighter does possibly the more realistic things to do in a scrap, and bring in backup.

This is a list of quantity vs quality, bringing into the spotlight a host of characters who prefer to take up a fight with numbers by their side. To make things clear, this is only counting characters who have their partner as a vital part of their moveset, so only bringing in a teammate for one or two attacks will not count here.

10/10 Rosalina & Luma | Super Smash Bros.

Smash Brothers is no stranger to team characters, being sure to have at least one in every game since Melee. But perhaps to the joy of certain Melee fans with vivid flashbacks of the Ice Climbers, let's go over one of the other team characters in Smash Bros. Namely, Rosalina and Luma.

Working in a similar fashion to the Ice Climbers, Rosalina and Luma will mimic each other’s buttons. If the player does an up tilt with Rosalina, Luma will follow suit, making it possible to hit an opponent twice with a single command. What separates the two is how the player can make Luma move away from its partner to control two spaces at once.

9/10 Zato-1 | Guilty Gear

No matter what one thinks of the game, no one can deny that it features some of the more distinct characters in fighting games. A child who hits people in the head with an anchor and plays with a dolphin mid-fight? That’s May. A girl who disguises herself in a mech suit to look like a man? That’s obviously Kum. A kid with glasses who is literally fighting in his sleep? Bedman.

While there are quite a few characters who get the assistance of an ally, Zato-1 and his shadow-like summons take the center stage here. Half of Zato’s entire gameplan revolves around his shadow companion, Eddie, and the limitless possibilities centered around him.

At any point in the match, he can summon Eddie. This eerie dark being can be told to do a list of commands separate from his partner, letting the two create visually impressive combos that never seem to end.

8/10 Videl | Dragon Ball FighterZ

In Dragon Ball FighterZ, people are going to see characters who rank amongst the most powerful across the universe. So how is an ordinary human going to keep up with a roster of ridiculously strong heavy hitters? By bringing her husband into battle.

Introducing Gohan, the Great Saiyaman. Sure, Videl cannot shoot exploding energy blasts from her hands, but Gohan sure can. Much of her gameplay revolves around getting in the opponent's face and calling him in for backup to extend her combos.

7/10 Ferra & Torr | Mortal Kombat X

While they admittedly weren’t the most popular amongst players, the fact that these were two characters filling a slot on the roster made them stand out among the cast. The thing that makes these two stand out from the other team characters in the series is that they extend each other’s moves instead of waiting to be tagged in.

Their attacks include Torr using his partner as a human bat, chucking her at the opponent from one of three different angles or grabbing the opponent to let Ferra carve into their head or guts. Ferra can even surprise the opponent at the end of a string for a sneaky hit.

The only downside to this brutish duo is how easy it can be for their synergy to break. The moment Torr is knocked down, moves involving Ferra are removed until she gets back.

6/10 Pain | Naruto Shippuden: Ultimate Ninja Storm

Pain is one of the big villains of Naruto Shippuden. With a sympathetic goal similar to the main character’s but with a much more violent twist, Pain quickly cemented himself as a horrifying threat to face.

Of course, he has his signature almighty push that hits even when the opponent is behind him, but his most intimidating aspect is that he brings his entire team to help fight.

Each of his combo attacks summons a different member of the team while the main man himself looms menacingly behind them. Even his grab involves a team member teleporting in to feed the enemy to a large, sentient gate.

5/10 Cait & Sith | Chaos Code

A wise man once said, “I got you, fam!” and two warriors from Chaos Code embody this phrase. Chaos Code was a lesser-known fighting game released by Arc System Works.

Teamwork is the name of the game as the player controls both twins to pull off some really flashy combos. Many of their moves consist of bringing in the other sibling or using the other as a human weapon.

From helping the other out with a cartwheel that damages the opponent to spinning the other to let their legs hit the enemy, these twins are more than ready to bust some heads.

4/10 Jotaro Kujo | JoJo's Bizarre Adventure: Heritage for the Future

JoJo’s Bizarre Adventure is a series known for ultra-powerful warriors beating each other to a pulp with the literal embodiment of their fighting spirit. Naturally, this translates amazingly well into a fighting game.

With his Stand, Star Platinum, Jotaro is able to perform some pretty flashy combos, some of which even involve the Stand showing up on the other side of the opponent to trap the unlucky person in between them.

Then he has access to the famous time stop ability where the player can go wild with whatever creative combo they can think of. Jotaro and his Stand are series mascots for a reason.

3/10 Tomoka Minato | Dengeki Bunko: Fighting Climax

In the crossover fighting game, Fighting Climax, anime fans can finally live out their dream of having characters from different series clash together. In this crossover, aspiring basketball player Tomoka from Ro-Kyu-Bu offers some much-needed team play.

Tomoka is able to bring her friends to do a list of things, including sending a projectile basketball that drops on the opponent’s head, skidding on the ground to pop an enemy back off the floor, spiking the enemy back down, or passing the transformed opponent so Tomoka can dribble them to a hoop.

Tomoka and her friends are playing a completely different game in every sense of the word.

2/10 The Maids | Melty Blood

Melty Blood has recently boomed in popularity in the last few years. This means there are new fans but also means there are even more people who know why Hisui and Kohaku are included here.

Hisui and Kohaku can both be picked individually. This will allow them to unlock slightly more moves that the other twin would simply not allow in their presence. But when picked as a team, they become a nightmare of mixups, setups, and flashy combos.

In some instances, they can even make an unsafe command completely safe by tagging into the other twin. Add that with the optional third ally to worry about in the form of a tree monster the maids can summon, and these twins become a problem for any foe.

1/10 Squigly | Skullgirls

Skullgirls was a 2D hand-drawn fighting game released in 2012 and to this day, the characters still reign as some of the more unique in its genre. From a zombie catgirl who can take her head off and use it as a second player onscreen, to a wrestler using the severed hand of a dead monster as a weapon.

Squigly is one of the two undead characters in the game, but rather than removing her limbs, she is partnered with her fire-breathing pet, Leviathan, who weirdly decided to take refuge in her corpse before she was resurrected.

Leviathan is an ancient parasite that latches to a host. This one in particular is one of the more polite ones in the roster, meshing with the well-mannered Squigly to form some adorable and playful attack animations between them where a simple gesture is a code for “go get ’em.”

There are even a few animations that don’t truly look like they’re fighting at all, but simply enjoying each other’s company. The enemy just happens to be standing too close.
